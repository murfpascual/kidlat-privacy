
# Kidlat: Sparks of Ideas — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 2026

---

## Overview

Kidlat: Sparks of Ideas is a personal capture inbox app that lets you save links, notes, and screenshots from anywhere on your device. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a user.

---

## Data Collection

We collect the following categories of information:

- **Account details** — your name and email address, provided optionally through Sign in with Apple. Apple may anonymize or hide your email at your discretion.
- **User-generated content** — links, notes, screenshots, tags, and reminders you save within the app.
- **App preferences** — your chosen color theme and notification settings, stored locally on your device.

We do not collect location data, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or any data unrelated to the core function of the app.

---

## Data Usage

Your information is used exclusively to operate the app's features — saving, organizing, searching, and reminding you of your captured content. Your saved items are private to your account and are never shared with other users or third parties.

**We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising purposes.**

---

## Third-Party Partners

- **Supabase** — handles authentication and cloud storage of your saved items and tags. Data is stored securely on Supabase-managed servers. See [supabase.com/privacy](https://supabase.com/privacy).
- **Apple Sign in** — authentication is handled entirely by Apple. We receive only the account identifier and optional name/email that Apple provides.

Biometric authentication (Face ID / Touch ID) is handled entirely by iOS. Your biometric data never leaves your device and is never accessible to Kidlat.

---

## Data Retention & Deletion

Your data remains stored while your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the address below. Local app data (folder structure, preferences) can be removed by uninstalling the app.

---

## Child Safety

Kidlat is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

---

## Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
